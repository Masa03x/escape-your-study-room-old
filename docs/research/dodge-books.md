# Research: dodging flying books

Status: research plan only. No findings recorded yet.

## Main question

How can I make a short movement-based book-dodging challenge that students understand and enjoy during a study break?

## Questions and methods

| Question | How I will investigate it |
| --- | --- |
| Do students understand the challenge and its purpose? | Show the concept and ask them to explain it in their own words. |
| Is the movement and phone position clear and comfortable? | Observe a prototype session and ask about comfort and screen visibility. |
| Can phone motion reliably trigger a dodge? | Try sensor readings on a real phone and compare intended movements with ordinary handling. |
| Are the feedback and ending clear? | Observe whether students notice counted movements and completion. |
| Does the humour fit the break? | Ask which parts felt playful, confusing or distracting. |

## Technical experiment plan

After choosing the framework, inspect current official sensor documentation and record the exact dependency version used. Compare readings while standing still, making the intended movement and handling the phone normally. Record phone model, phone position, detection settings, expected movements, accepted detections, missed movements and false detections.

Choose thresholds and timing from these observations. Do not claim that phone motion verifies squat technique.
