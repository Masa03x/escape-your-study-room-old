# User-test plan: dodging flying books

Status: planned. No sessions or results recorded yet.

## Goal

Find out whether students understand the challenge, know how to move and hold the phone, notice the feedback and understand when the break ends.

## Round 1: concept prototype

Aim to test with three ICT students as a small exploratory test. Explain that the design is being tested and that they can stop at any time. Use anonymous participant IDs.

Ask each participant to:

1. Start the challenge without being guided through the interface.
2. Explain the instructions and how they would hold the phone.
3. Try the movement if comfortable, with enough space.
4. Explain whether a dodge counted and how they know.
5. Pause, resume and leave the challenge.
6. Reach the ending and explain what they would do next.

If movement is simulated, explain that limitation and test the concept only. Do not report simulated input as working detection.

Ask afterwards:

- What was clear or confusing?
- Was it comfortable to move while holding and viewing the phone?
- Did the challenge feel like a useful break or another task?
- Was anything funny? Did anything feel annoying?
- What would you change?

## Round 2: implemented feature

Repeat the flow on a physical phone after making improvements. Also observe missed movements, false detections, repeated counts, pause/resume behaviour and completion. Record the device and app commit used.
